For parents

Questions parents ask us

Sending your child abroad is a big decision. Here's what you'll want to know first.

In safe hands

Our safeguarding policy follows Volleyball England guidance. Every member of staff on a trip is DBS checked, and a named welfare officer is on hand before, during and after.

Who looks after my child on a trip?
England North coaching and welfare staff travel with the group and stay with them throughout. You'll get the staff list, the itinerary and an emergency contact number before you travel.
How much does it cost, and what's included?
Each programme card shows its price once it's set. We'll always give you a full breakdown of what's included, such as travel, accommodation, food, coaching and kit, before you commit to anything.
How are players selected?
Some programmes are open to any player in the age group; others are by trial. Each programme card says which. Register interest and we'll tell you exactly how it works for your child.
How do I pay?
Once a place is confirmed you'll get a deposit request, with the balance split into instalments before travel.
What about medical needs?
We collect medical and dietary details privately once a place is confirmed, never through the interest form. The welfare officer goes through them with you.

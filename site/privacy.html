Privacy notice

Last updated November 2026

England North Volleyball runs camps, tours and trials for volleyball players. This notice explains what we collect when you register interest or book a place, why we need it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

When you register interest: the player's name, date of birth, club and position, the programme they're interested in, a parent or guardian's name (for players under 18), and a mobile number and email address.

When a place is confirmed, we ask separately for emergency contacts, kit sizes, medical and dietary needs, and photo consent. We never collect medical details through the registration form on this website.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, to tell you about the programme you chose, to organise travel, accommodation and coaching, and to keep players safe while they're with us.

Who can see it

Only England North coaching and welfare staff who need it to run the programme. We use trusted services to store it (our website host and Google), and we never sell or share your details for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep registration details for as long as you're involved with England North, and delete them within two years of your last contact with us, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you or your child, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email camps@englandnorthvolleyball.co.uk (camps@englandnorthvolleyball.co.uk) and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Photos and video

We only use photos or video of a player on this website or our social media where we have consent from the player, or from a parent or guardian if they're under 18. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us.
